I am a first-year [MIIS](https://lti.cmu.edu/academics/masters-programs/miis.html) student in the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University, and I'm excited to join the [MSP lab](https://lab-msp.com/) under the supervision of [Prof. Carlos Busso](https://carlosbusso.com/).

Previously, I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in Taiwan. As an undergraduate, I conducted research under the supervision of [Prof. Hung-Yi Lee](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/~hylee/), focusing on natural langiage processing and multimodal machine learning.
